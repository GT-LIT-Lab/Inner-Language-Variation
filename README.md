\
Krempel*, R., Li*, J., Gao*, R., Yu, R., Ivanova, A.A. (accepted). Variations in Inner Language and Conceptual Thought Experience. Proceedings of the Annual Meeting of the Cognitive Science Society.

- Figures_CogSci_042026: Figures in the manuscript
- data_V1: Raw survey data used in the manuscript
- Infographics: Infographic instruction shown to participants to explain different forms of thinking
- Forms_of_Inner_Thinking (FIT)_v202604.pdf: Text version of the full questionnaire
